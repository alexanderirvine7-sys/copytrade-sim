How it works

  • A program watches pump.fun for new coins launched by one particular operator (wallet "EtCdHc" and the wallets that fund it).
  • When it spots one, it pretends to buy 0.5 SOL worth using three fixed rules, using real trade prices from the blockchain:
    • D1: buy 15 minutes after launch, sell 20 minutes later.
    • D2: buy 10 minutes after launch, sell 30 minutes later.
    • Q1: from 5 minutes after launch, wait until the operator and his wallets have sold less than 1 SOL in the last 2 minutes, then buy; sell 20 minutes later. If that never happens within the first hour, Q1 skips the coin.
  • A setup is also skipped if nobody traded the coin within 2 minutes of the planned buy time.
  • It follows each coin's price for 2 hours to record the peak, how long it took to get there, and when it crashed.
  • The totals are compared with the backtest. The backtest is small (34 coins from one operator) and its worst trades lost about 100%, so a few launches can swing the numbers a lot.
  • Coins that look similar but aren't from this operator are counted as "lookalikes" and not traded.
  • This page refreshes itself every minute; the data behind it is updated about every 5 minutes. All times are UK time.
