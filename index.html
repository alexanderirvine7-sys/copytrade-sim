Rules and caveats

  • A "Pure copy": the original account. Rules below. B and C were started at 10:31 GMT on 25 Oct by cloning A's state (SUI at A's cost basis, $52 start), so before then their balances equal A's.
  • B "Profit-taking": same entries as A (0.25x of the trader's USD size, capped at B's own cash, same costs). Exits per position, whichever comes first: at +100% from average entry sell half; on the rest a 30% trailing stop from the peak since entry; price falls 20% or more within about 5 minutes: sell everything; trader sells: same trim rule as A (below). Prices are checked every ~15 s.
  • C "Sell all at 2x": same entries as A. Sells the whole position at +100% from average entry; trader sells follow the trim rule below. No trailing stop and no fast-drop exit.
  • D: buys coins that appear on his pump.fun page, 20% of cash, sells when he sells or at +100%. Started with $52 at 11:27 GMT on 25 Oct, separate from A, B and C. Signal: a coin newly appearing on slingoor's pump.fun profile (a new holding worth $5+, a coin he replies on, or a coin he creates), checked every ~3 minutes; coins already there at the start are ignored. A new holding must show up in two checks in a row (so about 3-6 minutes after it appears) because pump.fun's holdings list can briefly drop and re-add low-value coins. Buy: 20% of D's cash (min $1; under $1 is skipped as "no cash") at the live price (Jupiter, fallback DexScreener) with the same fees and slippage as the others; coins with no price or no liquidity data are skipped. Exit, all at once: at +100% from D's entry (price checked every ~15 s), or when his on-chain balance of that coin falls 5% or more between checks (~20 s); smaller trims are ignored unless they add up to over half of his balance at D's entry. For coins he only replied on or created and never held, only the +100% exit applies until he holds some.
  • After an exit an account holds cash and waits. On the trader's next buy each account copies 0.25x capped at its own cash, so the accounts diverge (A at $0 cash skips buys that B and C take). Buying a fully exited token again is a fresh entry; adding to a held remainder recomputes the average entry and resets the trailing peak (and re-arms B's half-sell). Average entry includes fees and slippage. Trader switches are decided by A's results only; B and C follow the same trader.
  • Buys copy 0.25x the trader's USD size, capped at our cash. A buy is skipped if cash is under $0.50.
  • 30-second execution delay (from 25 Oct, all accounts): every copy buy executes 30 s after the leader's buy at the live price then (Jupiter, fallback DexScreener), plus fees and slippage. If the leader has already fully sold the token by then, the buy is skipped ("skipped: leader flipped within 30s").
  • Late buys: if we see a buy more than 30 s late we buy at once at the live price, except when we see it more than 5 minutes late and the price is more than 50% above the leader's entry price (his SOL/USDC spent ÷ tokens received): then it is skipped ("skipped: late and pumped").
  • Sells and small trims (from 25 Oct, all accounts): if the leader sells less than 5% of his position in a token (tokens sold ÷ his balance just before), nobody sells ("ignored: leader trim <5%"). If those small trims since our entry add up to more than 50% of his position at our entry, we sell the same total fraction. Sells of 5% or more are mirrored proportionally (he sells 30% of his tokens: we sell 30% of ours); if he exits fully we sell everything. Before 25 Oct any sell closed our whole position.
  • Sell fills use the trader's implied price if we see the sell within 90 s, otherwise the live price at detection.
  • Two losing round trips in a row: close everything and switch to the next-best weekly trader.
  • Ranking comes from Kolscan's public weekly leaderboard, not the FOMO app, so the trader may differ from the original post.
  • Detection (from 25 Oct): the leader's newest transactions are polled every ~5 s (with a 60 s gap check and a token-balance check each cycle), so most trades are seen within seconds; copies still lag by the 30 s execution delay and memecoin prices move within seconds. Before 25 Oct the poll was ~60 s.
  • Costs (from 25 Oct, on every new fill): 1% swap fee per side plus slippage = 1% base + a liquidity tier from DexScreener pool liquidity (L ≥ $500k +0; $100k–500k +0.5%; $25k–100k +1%; $5k–25k +2.5%; under $5k or unknown +5%) + 2 × trade size / L, capped at 15%. Fills before then had no costs. Real costs (priority fees, MEV, failed swaps) could still be worse.
  • The free public Solana feed can lag or miss trades on very busy wallets; trades quoted in tokens other than SOL, USDC or USDT are skipped.
  • The Kolscan weekly leaderboard is re-read daily and at every switch, so a switch goes to the current next-best trader not yet dropped.
  • Prices from Jupiter (fallback DexScreener) are fetched on the box at each push (at most every 5 minutes, to stay within GitHub Pages limits). This page re-checks every 60 s.
